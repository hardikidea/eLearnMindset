# Academic Year Rollover

Users are reused. Do not re-register students, parents or teachers for the next year.

Year rollover changes:

- new courses
- new cohorts
- new groups
- new enrolments
- new teacher role assignments
- new cohort membership for promoted students
- academic history rows

Generate another future year:

```bash
./scripts/generate-next-year.sh 2029-2030 2030-2031
```

Promotion plans are review files. Grade 10 stream selection and Grade 12 alumni handling require manual review.
