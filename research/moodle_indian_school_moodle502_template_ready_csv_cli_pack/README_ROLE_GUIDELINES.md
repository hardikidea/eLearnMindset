# Role Guidelines

## Trustee Manager

Assign to the Trust category or System context only when necessary. This role is cloned from Manager in the CLI script. Use it for non-technical management oversight, reports and category/course governance.

Do not give Site Administrator rights unless the trustee is responsible for Moodle technical administration.

## Principal

Assign to the School category. The principal should see and manage only the school area unless the same person controls multiple schools.

## Teacher

Use Moodle built-in editingteacher at the subject course level. For division-specific separation, put teachers and students in the relevant Division group and force separate groups in the course.

## Student

Use Moodle built-in student role through cohort sync enrolments. Do not create one role per class or division.

## Parent

Use parent role in the student user context. Do not enrol parents as students in child courses. Parent-child mapping is in 24_parent_links.csv.
