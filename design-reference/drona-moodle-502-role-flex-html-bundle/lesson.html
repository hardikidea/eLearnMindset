Major perspectives in psychology

Estimated reading time: 12 minutes

Psychology uses several perspectives to explain thought, emotion and behaviour.

Behavioural perspective

This perspective emphasises observable behaviour and learning through interaction with the environment.

Cognitive perspective

This perspective examines attention, memory, language, reasoning and problem-solving.
